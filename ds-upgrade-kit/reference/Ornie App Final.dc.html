A MODULE IS JUST A MANIFEST
name: pull-requests
source: api.github.com
rules:
- when: review_requested
then: task in "Website redesign", due same-day
- when: issue.label == "bug"
then: card in Inbox
blocks:
- open-prs # available in Pages via /
sidebar: true
Modules see only what their rules touch — nothing else leaves the service. Uninstalling removes the module's screens; anything it filed into your workspace stays yours.
